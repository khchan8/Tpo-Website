Strength, Power & Recovery

The Peak Performance Line is TPO Wellness' training-focused gummy collection, formulated and manufactured in our Thai FDA approved, GMP certified Bangkok facility. Two flagship formulas cover the full workout window — explosive energy before training and clean muscle repair after it.

  • 3–5g micronized creatine monohydrate per serving with high bioavailability
  • Bioactive collagen peptides with 2:1:1 BCAAs and a full EAA profile
  • Smooth pectin bite with no gritty or chalky texture
  • OEM and white-label production from 3,000 units (pilot MOQ)

Creatine Power Gummies (FORM-CR01)

Micronized creatine monohydrate suspended in a smooth heat-stable vegan pectin matrix, delivering 3–5g of creatine per serving (up to 5,000mg per 4-gummy serving) for strength and cellular ATP output. Available in Berry Burst and Citrus Burst flavors, with vitamin B12 and electrolytes to support training sessions. The micronized, high-bioavailability formulation dissolves cleanly — no gritty sediment and zero chalky aftertaste.

Pro-Collagen & Amino Protein Gummies (FORM-PR02)

Bioactive collagen peptides paired with 2:1:1 BCAAs (L-Leucine, L-Isoleucine, L-Valine) and essential amino acids (EAAs) for post-training recovery — 12g of protein and amino actives per pack with no chalky shake residue. Finished in a Tropical Mango flavor with vitamin C to support collagen formation, in a gelatin or vegan pectin hybrid base.
