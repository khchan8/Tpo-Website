Expert Herbal Product Manufacturing Services

OEM & white-label solutions for herbal teas, standardized botanical extracts, functional mushroom blends, tinctures, and traditional Plai balms in Thailand.

Why TPO Wellness for Your Herbal Products?

Herbal wellness continues to grow worldwide, and Thailand's botanical heritage gives your brand an authentic edge. Delivering a consistently high-quality product is key to building brand loyalty. TPO Wellness offers reliable production for a full range of herbal formats, backed by stringent quality control and our licensed Thai Traditional Medicine practice.

  • Thai FDA Approved & GMP Certified Facility: Manufacturing under the highest safety and quality protocols.
  • Thai Traditional Medicine Expertise: Formulation support informed by licensed TTM physicians and USA-trained production specialists.
  • Focus on Purity, Consistency & Compliance: Standardized botanicals and verified batch quality, every run.
  • Build Your Brand: Partner with us for dependable inventory and enhanced brand reputation.

Herbal Production Capabilities & Specifications

We specialize in producing a range of popular herbal product formats:

  • Types Offered: Herbal Teas (loose-leaf and pyramid sachets), Standardized Botanical Extracts, Functional Mushroom Blends (Lion's Mane, Reishi, Chaga), Herbal Tinctures, and Traditional Plai Balms.
  • Available Formats:
    • Herbal Teas: loose-leaf pouches and single-serve sachets
    • Extracts & Mushroom Blends: standardized powders and dual-extracted (hot-water and alcohol) concentrates
    • Tinctures & Balms: 30ml/60ml dropper bottles and 30g/60g balm jars
  • Formulation & Enhancement Options: Single-herb or custom multi-botanical blends, marker-compound standardization, and custom aromatic profiles.
  • Sourcing Options: Choose from TPO-cultivated Thai botanicals, client-supplied herbs, or custom-developed proprietary blends.

Customization & Packaging Solutions

Tailor your herbal products to match your brand's specific needs.

  • Product Customization: Options include botanical selection (TPO-cultivated or client-supplied), blend creation, extract standardization levels, and aromatic profiles.
  • Packaging Flexibility:
    • Available Formats: We offer packaging in stand-up pouches, tins, amber glass bottles, and jars (with custom label options).
    • Client-Supplied: We accept and utilize your provided packaging solutions.

Quality Assurance & Compliance

Our commitment to quality ensures superior herbal products:

  • Certifications: Produced in our Thai FDA Approved and GMP Certified facility.
  • Strict Quality Control: We implement measures to ensure consistency in botanical identity, extract standardization, fill weight, and seal integrity.
  • Testing Protocols: Commitment to thorough quality and potency testing, with a Certificate of Analysis (CoA) available per batch.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Strict adherence to all relevant Thai FDA herbal product regulations.
  • Material Sourcing: Botanicals are cultivated and sourced through a documented, traceable Thai supply chain.

The TPO Manufacturing Process

Our efficient process ensures a smooth path from concept to delivery:

  1. Inquiry & Consultation: Define your herbal product specifications and project scope.
  2. Botanical & Formula Confirmation: Finalize botanical materials, extraction methods, and packaging components.
  3. Production: Execute extraction, blending, filling, and finishing stages.
  4. Quality Control: Perform in-process and final QC checks, including CoA testing.
  5. Packaging: Package herbal products according to specifications.
  6. Distribution (Optional): Coordinate delivery through our logistics service.

Logistics & Distribution Service
