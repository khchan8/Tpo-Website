OEM Supplement Gummy Manufacturing in Thailand

Turnkey white-label gummy OEM from TPO Wellness: pectin and gelatin bases, a Thai FDA & GMP certified facility, CoA lab testing, custom flavors and sweeteners, packaging, and Thai FDA registration support.

Why Choose TPO Wellness for Your Gummies?

The demand for high-quality supplement gummies keeps growing. Partnering with an experienced manufacturer is crucial for brand success. TPO Wellness combines expertise in gummy formulation and production with state-of-the-art facilities to deliver exceptional products.

  • Thai FDA Approved & GMP Certified Facility: Ensuring the highest standards of safety and quality.
  • Expert Team Trained by USA Nutraceutical Professionals: International knowledge applied locally.
  • One-Stop Shop: Streamlined R&D, production, packaging, and registration.
  • Focus on Quality & Consistency: CoA-tested, reliable products batch after batch.
  • Your Success Partner: Achieve faster speed-to-market and ensure brand consistency.

Gummy Manufacturing Capabilities & Specifications

We formulate and manufacture premium supplement gummies tailored to your brand's needs. Our core capabilities include:

  • Creatine Gummies: Creatine Power Gummies for pre-workout and performance brands, available in Berry and Citrus flavor profiles.
  • Protein & Amino Gummies: Pro-Collagen & Amino Protein Gummies with collagen peptides and essential amino acids, in Tropical Mango.
  • Sleep Gummies: Deep Slumber Sleep Gummies with L-Theanine, Magnesium, and calming botanicals, in Mixed Berry.
  • Relax Gummies: Zen Botanica Relax Gummies with Ashwagandha and L-Theanine, in Passionfruit Yuzu.
  • Custom Formulations: Bespoke active blends built around your target profile, with pectin or gelatin bases and custom flavor and sweetener systems (including sugar-free options).
  • Turnkey Branding, Packaging & Registration: Custom shapes, colors, and packaging plus Thai FDA product registration handled end-to-end for your brand.

Customization & Packaging Solutions

Create gummies that perfectly represent your brand identity.

  • Packaging Flexibility:
    • Client-Supplied: We readily accept and work with your pre-sourced packaging.

Quality Assurance & Compliance

Quality is paramount at TPO Wellness. We operate under strict protocols:

  • Certifications: Our facility is Thai FDA Approved and GMP Certified.
  • Rigorous Testing: Every production batch is laboratory tested and released with a Certificate of Analysis (CoA) covering active content, purity, and microbiological safety.
  • Regulatory Adherence: We strictly follow all Thai FDA food and dietary supplement regulations, and support your product registration filing.
  • Ingredient Integrity: Actives and raw materials are sourced through a documented, traceable supply chain with allergen controls.

The TPO Manufacturing Process

We offer a streamlined process from concept to completion:

  1. Consultation: Discuss your project requirements, goals, and vision.
  2. Formulation & Sampling: Develop and refine your unique gummy formula, providing samples for approval.
  3. Pilot Production: Run your launch batch (MOQ 3,000–5,000 units) in our certified facility.
  4. Quality Assurance: Conduct CoA lab testing throughout and post-production.
  5. Packaging: Package your finished products according to your requirements.
  6. Distribution (Optional): Arrange for delivery via our in-house logistics service.
