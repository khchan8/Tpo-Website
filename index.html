About TPO Wellness Ltd. — Bangkok

Contract Gummy Manufacturing Rooted in Food Science and Thai Botanical Practice

Based in Bangkok, TPO Wellness Ltd. works with supplement brands, gym chains, and regional distributors to develop shelf-stable functional gummies and herbal formulations. Every batch is produced in-house under Thai FDA and Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) standards.

  • Tropical Heat-Resistant Gummy Base: High-methoxyl vegan pectin and hybrid matrices engineered to hold texture up to 40°C without sticking or sweating in transit.
  • High-Load Active Masking: Proprietary bitterness masking for high-dose Creatine Monohydrate, Collagen/BCAA peptides, minerals, and standardized Thai botanical extracts.
  • Full Regulatory & Lab Documentation: Certificate of Analysis (CoA), microbial and heavy-metal testing, Thai FDA registration (SorBor), and export certificates.
