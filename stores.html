Our Bangkok Locations

TPO Wellness Ltd. operates three locations in and around Bangkok — a Thai FDA approved & GMP certified gummy factory and laboratory, our flagship herbal retail shop, and a licensed Thai Traditional Medicine clinic.

01 / Manufacturing

OEM Gummy Factory & Lab

Thai FDA approved & GMP certified contract manufacturing facility for pectin and gelatin supplement gummies, including in-house R&D and quality-control laboratory.

Address: Bangkok Metropolitan Area, Thailand (exact site shared with confirmed clients)

Hours: Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00 ICT

Visits: B2B client audits by appointment only

Book an Audit Appointment

02 / Retail

TPO Herbal Shop

Our retail shop in central Bangkok stocks standardized Thai botanical extracts, mushroom tinctures (Lion's Mane, Reishi, Chaga), herbal teas, and small-batch wellness gummies produced in our GMP facility.

Address: Central Bangkok, Thailand

Hours: Daily 10:00–20:00 ICT

Also available: Wholesale supply for retail partners

View on Google Maps

03 / Clinical Care

TPO Thai Traditional Clinic

Licensed Thai Traditional Medicine (TTM) physicians offering diagnostic consultations and compounded herbal prescriptions, including sleep, stress, and digestive-care programs.

Address: Bangkok, Thailand

Hours: Tue–Sun 10:00–19:00 ICT (closed Monday)

Appointments: Consultations by licensed TTM physicians
