Why the Herbal & Traditional Line?

We start with standardized Thai botanical extracts and functional mushroom blends — Lion's Mane, Reishi, and Chaga — sourced from verified Thai growers and processed in our Thai FDA–registered, GMP-certified facility in Bangkok: identity-verified, purity-tested botanicals with consistent extract concentration. Flagship formulas include Mushroom Defense Immune Shield, Reishi Restore Recovery Blend, and Chill Chaga Nighttime Tonic. And before products reach the TPO Herbal Shop or our retail partners, we ensure they are up to standard through third-party Certificate of Analysis (COA) testing.
